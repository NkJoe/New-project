About Open Heavens Parish

Cathedral of Champions

Our Story

Open Heavens Parish is a vibrant congregation of the Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG), established to spread the gospel and make disciples in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State. Our journey began with a vision to create a place where people can experience the love of Christ and grow in their faith.

We are committed to excellence in worship, teaching, and service, creating an environment where individuals and families can thrive spiritually and build meaningful relationships within our community.

Our Mission

To spread the gospel of Jesus Christ, make disciples, and transform lives through the power of God's Word and the Holy Spirit. We are committed to reaching the lost, building believers, and serving our community with love and compassion.

Our Vision

To be a leading church in Uyo, known for spiritual excellence, community impact, and raising champions for Christ. We envision a church where every member is equipped to fulfill their God-given purpose and make a difference in the world.

About RCCG

Redeemed Christian Church of God

The Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG) is a global church with headquarters in Lagos, Nigeria. Founded in 1952 by Rev. Josiah Akindayomi, the church has grown to become one of the largest Pentecostal denominations in the world.

RCCG is known for its emphasis on holiness, righteousness, and the power of the Holy Spirit. The church operates under the leadership of Pastor Enoch Adejare Adeboye, the General Overseer, and has established churches in over 190 countries worldwide.
